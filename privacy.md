# Privacy Policy for La Línea

Last updated: [03/15/2026]

## Introduction

La Línea helps users track border wait times between the United States and Mexico through community reports and location-based session tracking.

This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect and how we use it.

---

## Information We Collect

### Location Data

La Línea may collect location information when you start a border crossing session. This data is used to estimate wait times and improve accuracy for other users.

Location data is only used for wait-time calculations and is not sold or shared with third parties.

---

### Account Information

When you create an account we may collect:

- Username
- User ID
- Language preference
- XP and participation statistics

---

### 📸 Camera and Photos
We may request access to your device camera and photos to allow you to upload images within the app.

- Images are only accessed when you choose to upload them
- We do not access your camera or photos without your interaction
- Uploaded images may be used in community posts or reports

---

### 🖼️ Media Access
The app may access your device’s photo library only when you choose to upload content.

- We do not scan or access your media without your action

---

### Profile Photos

Users may upload a profile picture. Profile images are stored securely using Firebase Storage.

---

### Community Content

Users can create:

- Threads
- Comments

This content may be visible to other users of the app.

---

### Device Information

We may collect limited technical information such as:

- Device type
- App version
- Crash logs

This information helps us improve stability and performance.

---

## How We Use Information

We use collected information to:

- Calculate border wait times
- Provide community discussion features
- Improve app performance
- Prevent abuse of the system

---

## Data Storage

La Línea uses Google Firebase services for backend infrastructure including:

- Firebase Firestore (database)
- Firebase Authentication
- Firebase Storage (profile pictures)

---

## Data Sharing

We do **not sell user data to third parties**.

Some information such as community posts may be visible to other users of the app.

---

## Data Retention

Data may be retained to maintain historical border wait-time statistics and community discussions.

---

## Children's Privacy

La Línea is not intended for children under 13.

---

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. Changes will be reflected on this page.
